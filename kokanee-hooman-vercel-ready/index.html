THE KOKANEE HOOMAN WAY

Real ingredients. Real cooking.
No shortcuts.

Why we ask for 1 hour

Some commercial kitchens prepare curry bases and gravies in advance, then mix and reheat them to serve meals quickly. We choose to prepare our food fresh when you order. It takes longer, but we believe it makes a difference to flavour and the care that goes into your meal.

No artificial food colours. No packaged masala mixes. We aim to minimise unnecessary processed ingredients wherever practical. Please order at least 1 hour before you want your meal.

Fresh preparationCooked for your order, not assembled from ready-made gravies.

FROM OUR SOIL TO YOUR PLATE

Growing towards a better kitchen

We've started growing nachani (finger millet) and rice. We are also beginning to use groundnut oil in selected preparations and coconut oil from our own coconut trees where available.

Today, most dishes still use commercially available refined oil. Our goal is to gradually move towards more natural, traditionally pressed oils, grow more spices on our farm, and prepare more ingredients ourselves.

Our bigger ambition is a more self-reliant, sustainable kitchen — and to help raise everyday standards of hygienic food preparation in Goa, one small, consistent step at a time.

We're not there yet. We're working towards it, honestly.

LESS WASTE, CLEANER GOA

Bring your own dabba ♻

Picking up your order or ordering regularly? Bring your own clean tiffin or reusable container whenever possible to help us reduce disposable packaging and cutlery.

You can also ask about borrowing one of our reusable tiffin dabbas for a small refundable deposit, and return it when convenient. Availability and deposit amount will be confirmed by the kitchen.

Our shared goal is simple: keep Goa and our surroundings clean.

BE PART OF THE CHANGE

Cook, share, and help us improve

Interested in cooking classes for women? Our chef is a woman, and we currently offer classes for women only. Email us to enquire about availability.

Have ideas for cleaner, more sustainable or more hygienic cooking? We'd love to hear them at kokaneehooman@gmail.com.

If you've tried our food, please leave us an honest Google review. Your feedback helps our small kitchen grow and encourages this approach to freshly prepared, hygienic food.
