THE KOKANEE HOOMAN WAY

Real ingredients.
Real cooking.
No shortcuts.

In many commercial kitchens, food is prepared using pre-cooked gravies, stored curry bases or ready-made mixtures that are reheated when an order arrives. We want to offer a different approach.

At Kokanee Hooman, we prepare our food from scratch rather than simply mixing and reheating pre-made bases. We believe freshly prepared food brings better flavour, freshness and care to every plate.

OUR JOURNEY TOWARDS SUSTAINABILITY

From our soil to your plate.

We want to grow, prepare and process more of the ingredients we use, with a closer connection to our land and fewer unnecessary commercial inputs.

🌾

What we've already started

Growing our own nachani (finger millet) and rice. We're also beginning to use groundnut oil and coconut oil from our own coconut trees in selected preparations.

🥥

Our current cooking oils

We currently use commercially available refined oil in most dishes. We want to gradually move towards more natural, traditionally pressed cooking oils.

🌱

Our future goals

Grow more spices on our farm, process more of our own masalas from raw ingredients, increase our use of traditionally pressed oils, and reduce dependence on commercially processed ingredients.

✧

Better food standards in Goa

Through small, consistent efforts, we hope to encourage more hygienic food preparation, responsible ingredient choices and sustainable kitchen practices across Goa.

A journey, not a claim of perfection.
LESS WASTE, CLEANER SURROUNDINGS

Bring your own dabba. ♻

If you're a regular customer or collecting a pickup order, we encourage you to bring your own clean tiffin or dabba whenever possible. It's a simple way to reduce disposable packaging.

Need a reusable dabba?

You can request a reusable tiffin from us with a small refundable deposit and return it later. Ask us about availability when ordering.

Our goal is to reduce unnecessary waste and help keep Goa and our surrounding neighbourhoods clean.
